and client. Its pinned source, schemas, resources, fixtures, and checksum file
are retained under `src/Contracts/1.2`, `data/Contracts/1.2` and `tests/fixtures/Contracts/1.2/fixtures`. The provenance manifest records original paths, exact hashes and omitted SDK files. The unchanged upstream checksum list is `data/Contracts/1.2/UPSTREAM_SHA256SUMS`.

## Catalog and price services

The bundled outfit catalog identifies its source as the
[FFXIV Collect outfit API](https://ffxivcollect.com/api/outfits?limit=10000).
Other collection metadata is requested from [FFXIV Collect](https://ffxivcollect.com/).
Price estimates are requested from [Universalis](https://universalis.app/).
These services are independent of Collection Shopper.

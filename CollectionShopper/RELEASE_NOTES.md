# Collection Shopper 1.0.0.1

Unreleased source candidate.

- Find missing outfits, weapons, minions, mounts, orchestrion rolls, hairstyles,
  glasses, and fashion accessories beside the Market Board.
- Use Exportaramus 1.0.0.3 collection and storage observations to filter owned
  items. Items with unverified ownership stay unavailable for purchase.
- Check current-world Universalis estimates, with **Checking** and **Not Found**
  states when a usable price is not yet available.
- Purchase with **Buy** or **Buy All**, tax-inclusive per-item and per-run limits,
  optional HQ purchases, and a Stop control.
- Customize category tabs, purchase categories, tab style, and reduced motion.
- Inspect local readiness and copy a redacted report with
  `/collectionshopper diagnostics`.
